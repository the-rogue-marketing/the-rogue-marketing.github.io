Sam Altman famously predicted that we will soon see the first **one-person billion-dollar company**.

In 2026, the vanguard of that movement is already operating in plain sight: **Solo founders generating $5M to $15M in annual recurring revenue with zero full-time employees**.

Here is the operational and architectural blueprint behind the 1-Person Enterprise.

## 2. The Core Principles of Solo Leverage

1. **Ruthless Simplicity in the Core Stack**: No microservices architecture with 40 Kubernetes pods. A clean monolithic PostgreSQL database with a fast modern framework scales to tens of millions of dollars with near-zero DevOps maintenance.
2. **Automated Customer Support as a Tier 1 Asset**: When your RAG support agent is fed high-quality product documentation and given refund tools with strict rules, it resolves user issues within 5 seconds 24/7.
3. **Continuous Autonomous QA**: Run headless browser test suites (Playwright) on hourly cron jobs to catch customer UI breaks before users report them.
